About the artist

Craft, cultural memory and art education

My practice connects material skill with stories of family, community, nature and cultural inheritance.

Shugandhi Chowdhury Shraboni completed a Bachelor of Fine Art in Craft and a Master of Fine Art in Craft at the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh. Her recent studio work expands into watercolor and brush-pen drawing while continuing a long-standing engagement with handmade materials, Bengali cultural imagery and lived experience.

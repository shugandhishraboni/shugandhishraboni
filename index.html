Bangladesh · Craft · Cultural Memory · Art Education

Material stories, rooted in life.

Shugandhi Chowdhury Shraboni is a Bangladeshi craft artist and fine arts educator. Her practice spans watercolor, brush pen, wood, fiber, metal, screen print, collage, ceramic mosaic and installation.

About the artist

Craft, cultural memory and art education

My practice connects material skill with stories of family, community, nature and cultural inheritance.

Shugandhi Chowdhury Shraboni completed a Bachelor of Fine Art in Craft and a Master of Fine Art in Craft at the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh, and a Bachelor of Education from National University, Bangladesh. Her recent studio work expands into watercolor and brush-pen drawing while continuing a long-standing engagement with handmade materials, Bengali cultural imagery and lived experience.
